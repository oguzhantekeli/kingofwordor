What is deleted

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept in the live database: deletion is immediate. Encrypted daily backups expire on a rolling basis, so a deleted account can persist in a backup for up to 7 days, after which it is gone.

Progress stored only on your phone (as a guest) is not on our servers. Remove it by uninstalling the app, or with "Delete" on the profile screen.
